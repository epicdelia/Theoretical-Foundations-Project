FAQ

Questions, answered.

How is this different from just using ChatGPT?

ChatGPT is a smart assistant with no context. ChadGPT is grounded in your CRM, your won/lost deals, your ICP, and your prospects' real-time signals — so the output isn't generic, it's specific to your accounts and your motion. We also handle the workflow: research, drafting, syncing, coaching, forecasting.

Which CRMs do you support?

Native two-way sync with Salesforce and HubSpot. Pipedrive and Close are in beta. Custom CRMs are supported on the Enterprise plan via our sync API.

Will my data train a shared model?

No. Your data is isolated to your tenant and never used to train shared or third-party models. On Enterprise you can additionally bring your own keys and deploy a private model instance.

How long does implementation take?

Most teams are live in under 30 minutes. Enterprise rollouts with SSO and custom ICP work typically take 1–2 weeks with a dedicated CSM.

Do you have an SLA?

Yes. Growth includes 99.9% uptime; Enterprise includes 99.99% uptime, 24/7 support, and a named CSM.

Can we try before we buy?

Yes — every plan starts with a free 14-day trial. No credit card. You can connect your real CRM or a sandbox.
